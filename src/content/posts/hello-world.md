This is the first post on the new site. It's built with [Astro](https://astro.build), authored in plain Markdown, and published automatically on every push to GitHub.

Photos from [photo.longwalkhome.net](https://photo.longwalkhome.net) appear right in the home feed, interleaved with posts by date.

To write a new post, add a Markdown file to `src/content/posts/` with a `title` and `date` in the frontmatter, and push.
