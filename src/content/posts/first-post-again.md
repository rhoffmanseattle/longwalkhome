This is my obligatory "first blog post"… post.

Lost in the annals of time, back when blogging was "a thing," the first thing you would do, without question, was publish a "FIRST POST" post.

Here we are, with much water having passed under the bridge of Internet Protocol Bridge, and I feel like the tradition still has some legs.

I built this blog using two AI prompts and my trusty Netlify hosting plan. Git Repo is [here](https://github.com/rhoffmanseattle/longwalkhome). I chose not to use WordPress, mostly out of novelty and a desire to have something that loads instantly (time will tell) while also building something that aggregates content from other sources around the internet.
